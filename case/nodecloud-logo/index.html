Logo for NodeCloud, a service that rents out GPU servers. The mark is built from equal circles on one grid: nodes linked into a cloud.

Role

  • Logotype
  • Brand identity

Status

Done, the site is in development

NodeCloud logo

A node is a circle. Seven nodes are linked into two rings, and together they read as a cloud.
Construction
Every arc of the mark comes from one circle of diameter d.The circles stand on a grid of step d: the mark is 5 by 3 steps.The word is 1½ d tall, centred on the mark and ¾ d away from it.
Site icon
Clear space
The logo keeps a margin of x on every side, where x is one circle of the mark.
