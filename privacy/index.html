Who

This is the personal portfolio of Nurbol Bapanov, a designer in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan. Questions about this page: n.bapanov.54@gmail.com.

Data

Visits are counted by Yandex Metrica: which pages are opened, where visitors come from, clicks, scrolling and recordings of how a page is used (Webvisor). Yandex receives your IP address and details of your browser and device, and handles them under the Yandex Privacy Policy. I see only the reports, not who you are.

The site has no forms, no accounts and no advertising.

Cookies

The analytics service sets its own cookies to tell a new visit from a returning one. You can block them in your browser or with a content blocker: the site works the same without them.

Besides that, your browser keeps two short notes for the site, and they never leave your device: the language you chose, and that you closed the notice about cookies. For the length of one visit it may also note that it reloaded itself after an update.

Clearing the site data in your browser removes all of it.

Hosting

The pages are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor and stores it for security purposes: see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links

The contact links open Telegram, Instagram and your mail app, which follow their own rules. The CV is a PDF with my phone number and email, published so that people can reach me about work.

Fonts

Text is set in Arial where the device has it, and in Arimo where it does not. The Biamino and NodeCloud boards are set in Open Sans. Arimo and Open Sans are served by the site itself, with no third party, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: licence of Arimo, licence of Open Sans.

The Tochka boards name Helvetica Neue, a font of the device itself: nothing is downloaded for it.

Photos

The photographs on the ololoYurt website were provided by ololoYurt. In the Tochka prototype the barber portraits are AI-generated, and the haircut photos come from Pexels.
